A simple no-knead loaf

Mix flour, water, salt and a little yeast in a large bowl until no dry flour is left.

Cover the bowl and leave the dough to rise overnight at room temperature.

Shape it gently, bake it in a hot covered pot, then uncover it so the crust can brown.

Let the loaf cool on a rack for an hour before slicing.
