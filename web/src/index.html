Scam Pattern Ledger

A second Intelligent Contract, PatternLedger, keeps a public record of scam patterns, never the messages: a few fixed warning-sign codes, a category and a short summary. Before anything is stored, each GenLayer validator checks with its own AI model that the summary identifies no one and describes a real scam pattern. A reworded report of a known scam is counted as another sighting, not stored twice.
