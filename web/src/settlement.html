Settlement
Court.

Did they hold up their end? SettlementCourt is a GenLayer Intelligent Contract for handshake and freelance agreements. A claimant describes the deal and lists the promises; an AI jury rules on each one — kept, broken or disputed — and the contract derives one overall ruling. Validators each re-run the jury and the ruling is written only when they reach the same result. Everything below the explainer is read live from the contract by your browser.

  • Live read by your browser now
  • Verdict-only no money, no escrow

How it works

The court produces a public, checkable opinion about whether promises were kept. There are no payable methods, no escrow and no funds — nothing is settled financially on-chain.

How a ruling is reached

  • Per-deliverable labels. The jury labels each promise kept, broken or disputed on its own.
  • One derived ruling. The overall ruling — Fulfilled, Partially fulfilled, Not fulfilled or Undecided — is computed deterministically from the per-deliverable majority, not from the model's prose. That derivation is the consensus anchor every validator re-computes.
  • Bigger panels on appeal. Tier 1 is 3 jurors; an appeal re-hears with 5, then 7 independent jurors.
  • Appeals that change nothing are recorded. If a larger panel returns the same ruling, that round is marked upheld and the case's appeals_upheld tally rises — the public cost of a frivolous appeal.

Privacy and scope

  • No digit, "@", currency symbol, link or bare domain reaches the jury: the agreement and every deliverable pass the same PII screen as the scam ledger, and the redaction placeholders ([LINK], [NUMBER], [AMOUNT], …) stand in.
  • If the jury's majority says the redacted text still identifies a private person, the case is refused and nothing is stored.
  • A subject is a wallet address, never a name. A case may have none (advisory-only).
  • This is a testnet opinion for entertainment and tooling, not legal or financial advice.

Reputation lookup

Opt-in · read-only

ReputationLedger is an opt-in, address-only settlement track record. A scorecard exists for an address only after that address itself opts in; the ledger stores wallet addresses and bounded counts, never names or case text. Paste an address to read its scorecard with get_scorecard.

  • GenLayer docs Intelligent Contracts and the equivalence principle.

What this does NOT do

  • It moves no money. There is no escrow, no payment and no release of funds; the ruling is an opinion, not a settlement.
  • It scores no one involuntarily. A reputation scorecard exists only for an address that opted itself in, and the court never scores anyone on its own.
  • The overall ruling is derived by the contract from the per-deliverable majority. Whether each label is right is the judgement of the validators' AI models; models can be wrong.
  • This is GenLayer's studionet test network. No real value, no money, no tokens.
