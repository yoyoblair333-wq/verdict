Notice: the fall plant swap has moved to Sunday

The fall plant swap will now take place on Sunday, November 9, from 10 am to 1 pm at the garden gate.

It was first announced for Saturday, November 8, but the Saturday date was cancelled because the city marathon closes River Road that morning.

Bring cuttings, seedlings or seeds in labelled pots. Tea and coffee are provided by the garden committee.
